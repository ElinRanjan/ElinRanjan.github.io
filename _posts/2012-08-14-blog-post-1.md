Why am I writing on this?
======
I came across this paper first in late March, just around the time it got published in PNAS. It piqued my interest because of radical it sounded. The claims were very bold: that the Hilbert space quantum mechanics lives in is not continuous as we think it to be, rather it is highly discretized. I began to read the paper, and it was a very interesting experience. I want to divulge what I understood from my reading, as well as what makes it stand out from all the AI-generated slops out there in the world wide web.

What is Rational Quantum Mechanics?
======
Consider a one-qubit quantum mechanical state $\ket{\psi(\theta,\phi)}=\cos \frac{\theta}{2} \ket{0} + e^{i\phi} \sin \frac{\theta}{2} \ket{1}.$ This has 2 degrees of freedom, $\theta$ and $\phi$. These constitute a continuum of qubit states on the bloch sphere. However, the author now claims that these two parameters are not arbitrary. In fact, if I write the state instead as $\ket{\psi}=c_0\ket{0}+c_1\ket{1},$ then according to the author's claim, the Born probabilities are rational, and the relative phase is a rational fraction of $2\pi,$

$$
|c_0|^2 =\frac{m}{L} \in \mathbb{Q}, \qquad |c_1|^2 = 1 - |c_0|^2 \in \mathbb{Q}, \qquad \frac{1}{2\pi} \arg \left( \frac{c_1}{c_0} \right) = \frac{n}{L} \in \mathbb{Q},
$$

which means for $L \in \mathbb{N},$ the new discretization parameters $m$ and $n$ are bounded by $0\leq m,n \leq L.$ Based on two special cases of $L$ values, we get back classical mechanics ($L=1$) and quantum mechanics ($L=\infty$). Now things start to get really interesting when we consider a general $N$-qubit state. This has $2^N$ probability amplitude terms, each of which then contains two degrees of freedom. Then the total number of degrees of freedom is $2^{N+1}-2$, excluding the global phase and normalization.

The discretization allows us to express a one-qubit state as an $L-$ bit string, 

$$\ket{\psi} \equiv \\{ 0,0,0,\dots,0,0,0,1,1,1,\dots,1,1,1 \\} $$

where the number of $0$ is $m$, and the cyclic permutations of the string forms a cyclic group $C_n$.
