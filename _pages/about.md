I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at **NC State University**, advised by [Prof. Yuan Liu](https://yuanliu.group/) in the Quantum Engineering and Simulation Theory (QuEST) Lab and the [Center for Hybrid Quantum Computing](https://cvdv.ncsu.edu/). I am also a PhD research intern with the NextGen Architecture Team in the Future Computing Technologies Group at **Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL)**.

I develop **quantum algorithms for scientific computing and quantum chemistry**, with a focus on **hybrid oscillator–qubit (continuous–discrete-variable) architectures**, Hamiltonian simulation, and quantum solvers for differential equations.

Research directions
======

- **Hybrid oscillator–qubit algorithms for scientific computing.** Gate-level simulation of nonunitary linear dynamics and discretized PDEs, where a single oscillator mode replaces a register of ancilla qubits. At PNNL I co-design a simulation framework for nonlinear PDEs on superconducting-qubit hardware and study finite-resource implementations at scale on DOE supercomputers at NERSC.
- **Quantum simulation of molecular dynamics.** Quantum algorithms for conical-intersection dynamics in coupled vibronic systems, and a quantum ab initio multiple spawning framework for coupled electron–nuclear dynamics.
- **Molecular phase space and symmetry.** Symmetry-adapted oscillator–qubit representations of molecules, symmetry-fixed rovibrational and nuclear-spin states, and symmetry-protected molecular qudits for quantum computing.
- **Block-encodings and quantum signal processing** as a unifying framework for scalable quantum computational science.

I also collaborate with researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory on quantum algorithms for scientific computing.
